What are field effects?
什么是场地效果？
Field effects are special effects that affect the entire battlefield. Multiple field effects can be active at the same time.
场地效果是影响整个战场的特殊效果。多个场地效果可以同时生效。
Field Effect Duration
场地效果持续时间
Field effects last a certain number of turns. The length differs depending on what move or ability summoned the field effect. Some durations can be enhanced by certain items.
场地效果持续一定回合数。持续时间因召唤场地效果的招式或特性而异。某些道具可以延长持续时间。
Room Effects
房间效果
Puzzle Room, Odd Room and Polarized Room are rooms. Multiple can be active at the same time. Their duration can be doubled with the Reinforcing Rod item.
谜题房间、奇异房间和极化房间是房间效果。多个可以同时生效。它们的持续时间可以用强化棒道具翻倍。
Puzzle Room
谜题房间
Puzzle Room swaps each Pokémon's Attack and Sp. Attack stats.
谜题房间交换每只宝可梦的攻击和特攻能力值。
Odd Room
奇异房间
Odd Room swaps each Pokémon's Attack and Defense and Sp. Attack and Sp. Defense.
奇异房间交换每只宝可梦的攻击和防御、特攻和特防能力值。
Polarized Room
极化房间
Polarized Room raises super- and hyper-effective damage by 25% and lowers not-very and barely-effective damage by 25% of all Pokémon.
极化房间提升所有宝可梦超级有效和极有效伤害25%，降低不太有效和几乎有效伤害25%。
Gravity
重力
Gravity grounds all Pokémon, doubles their weight, and doubles all move Accuracy.
重力使所有宝可梦 grounded，体重翻倍，所有招式命中率翻倍。
Grey Mist
灰雾
Grey Mist resets all positive and negative stat changes back to neutral at the end of every turn.
灰雾在每回合结束时将所有正面和负面的能力变化重置为中性。
Floral Gramarye
花之魔法
Floral Gramarye heals every Pokémon on the field for 1/8th HP at the end of every turn.
花之魔法在每回合结束时为场上所有宝可梦恢复最大HP的1/8。
Side effects are special effects that affect only one side. Multiple side effects can be active at the same time.
副作用是影响单方的特殊效果。多个副作用可以同时生效。
Side effects last a certain number of turns. The length differs depending on what move or ability summoned the field effect. Some durations can be enhanced by certain items.
副作用持续一定回合数。持续时间因召唤场地效果的招式或特性而异。某些道具可以延长持续时间。
Screen Effects
屏障效果
Reflect, Light Screen, Aurora Veil, Sanctuary and Repulsion Field are screens. These effects do not stack with one another. Their duration can be enhanced with the Light Clay item.
反射壁、光墙、极光幕、庇护所和排斥力场是屏障效果。这些效果不会互相叠加。它们的持续时间可以用光之黏土道具延长。
Reflect
反射壁
Reflect lowers damage from incoming physical moves by 50% in a Singles battle or 33% in a Doubles/Triples battle.
反射壁在单打对战中降低受到的物理招式伤害50%，在双打/三打对战中降低33%。
Light Screen
光墙
Light Screen lowers damage from incoming special moves by 50% in a Singles battle or 33% in a Doubles/Triples battle.
光墙在单打对战中降低受到的特殊招式伤害50%，在双打/三打对战中降低33%。
Aurora Veil
极光幕
Aurora Veil lowers damage from incoming moves by 50% in a Singles battle or 33% in a Doubles/Triples battle. It can only be used during Hail.
极光幕在单打对战中降低受到的所有招式伤害50%，在双打/三打对战中降低33%。只能在冰雹天气下使用。
Sanctuary
庇护所
Sanctuary lowers damage from incoming moves by 25% in a Singles battle or 20% in a Doubles/Triples battle. It also prevents incoming critical hits.
庇护所在单打对战中降低受到的所有招式伤害25%，在双打/三打对战中降低20%。还可以防止受到会心一击。
Repulsion Field
排斥力场
Repulsion Field lowers damage from incoming moves with 100 BP or more by 50% in a Singles battle or 33% in a Doubles/Triples battle.
排斥力场在单打对战中降低受到威力100或以上的招式伤害50%，在双打/三打对战中降低33%。

誓约效果
Sea of Fire, Swamp and Rainbow are Pledge effects. They are acquired by using 2 different Pledge moves in the same turn.
火海、沼泽和彩虹是誓约效果。通过在同一回合使用两种不同的誓约招式获得。

火海通过组合火之誓约和草之誓约在对手一侧创造。非火属性宝可梦每回合结束时受到最大HP的1/8伤害。
